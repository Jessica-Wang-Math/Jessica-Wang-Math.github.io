Jessica Wang

I am a PhD student at Leibniz University Hannover. My advisor is Andreas Krug. I am currently working on semiorthogonal decompositions of derived categories of coherent sheaves.

I completed my master’s degree at Humboldt University of Berlin / Berlin Mathematical School, advised by Angela Ortega. I completed my bachelor’s degree at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, advised by Joseph Fehribach.
